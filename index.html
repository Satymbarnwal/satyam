About

I'm a student founder from India, and I build consumer software for the kind of everyday friction most people have stopped noticing.

The problem that started all of this found me in a queue in October 2025. Friends of mine missed a lecture waiting on a cafe counter, and on the same trip the network dropped while I was paying, so I lost my place in the line. The cafe already knew what it was going to cook. I already knew what I wanted and when I needed it. The only missing piece was a way to agree on the time beforehand.

That question turned into a product, and the product turned into a company. I founded ZAP Innovations in 2026 and now build across four areas, from food ordering to packaged water to an AI learning platform, with a software studio underneath that builds all of it.

I care most about the unglamorous part of this work: shipping to real people early, listening to the ones who complain, and designing for patchy networks and tight budgets because that is the normal case where I live, not the edge case.

The food was never the slow part. The line was.

Principles

How I work

Solve a problem you have had yourself

I lost my own lunch breaks to a queue before I wrote a line of code about it. It makes the first hundred decisions obvious.

Ship it before it is comfortable

A week in front of real people teaches you more than a month of planning in a document.

Both sides have to win

A cafe owner who cannot see their own peak hours stops opening the app by week two. The operator matters as much as the customer.

Build for India, properly

Patchy networks, UPI, tight budgets and crowded counters are the normal case here. Design for them first.
