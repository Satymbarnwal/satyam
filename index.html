About

Who I am

I'm Satyam Barnwal, founder and CEO of ZAP Innovations Pvt Ltd. I started it in 2026, in the middle of being a student, because I kept walking into problems that software should have solved a decade ago and somehow nobody had.

The first one found me in a queue, back in October 2025. Friends of mine missed a lecture waiting on a cafe counter, and on the same trip the network dropped while I was paying, so I lost my place in the line. The cafe already knew what it was cooking. I already knew what I wanted and when I needed it. The only missing piece was a way to agree on the time beforehand.

That became QueZap. It is one of four sectors we now build in, and the studio that builds our own products takes outside work too. The thread running through all of it is the same: take the part of a day that people waste, and hand it to software.

The food was never the slow part. The line was.
