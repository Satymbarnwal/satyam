About

Who I am

I'm Satyam Barnwal, founder and CEO of ZAP Innovations Pvt Ltd. I started the company in 2025, in the middle of being a student, because I kept walking into problems that software should have solved a decade ago and somehow nobody had.

The first one found me in a queue. Friends of mine missed a lecture waiting on a cafe counter, and on the same trip the network dropped while I was paying, so I lost my place in the line. The cafe already knew what it was cooking. I already knew what I wanted and when I needed it. The only missing piece was a way to agree on the time beforehand.

That became QueZap. It is the first product out of ZAP Innovations, not the last. What I'm actually interested in is consumer software for India, built by people who live with the same friction as the people using it.

The food was never the slow part. The line was.

02 — Flagship product

QueZap

Live

Pre-order food from partner cafes and campus canteens across India. Pay in advance, choose a collection slot, and walk straight to the counter. It is a pickup service, so there is no delivery fleet and no rider fee. Campus Mode is built around class timings, QuePoints rewards the regulars, and partner cafes get a dashboard showing live orders, peak hours and their best-selling items.

Beliefs

How I work

Solve a problem you have had yourself

I lost my own lunch breaks to this before I wrote a line of code about it. It makes the first hundred decisions obvious.

Ship it before it is comfortable

A product in front of real people for a week teaches you more than a month of planning it in a document.

The partner side is the product

A cafe owner who cannot see their own peak hours stops opening the app by week two. Both sides of the counter have to win.

Build for India, properly

Patchy networks, UPI, tight budgets and crowded counters are the normal case here, not the edge case. Design for them first.
