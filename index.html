How it started

The idea came out of an ordinary bad afternoon. Friends of mine missed a lecture because the queue at the cafe moved slower than the clock did. On the same trip the network dropped at the payment counter, and by the time it came back I had lost my place in the line.

Standing there, the maths was obvious. The cafe already knew what it was going to cook. I already knew what I wanted and when I needed it. The only thing missing was a way to agree on the time in advance.

I started researching that October, while still a student. ZAP Innovations Pvt Ltd came out of that research, and QueZap is the first thing we have built with it.

The food was never the slow part. The line was.
Product

QueZap

Pre-order food from partner cafes across India. Pay in advance, choose when you will collect, and skip straight to the counter. QueZap is a pickup service. There is no delivery fleet and no rider fee.

01

Pick a cafe

Browse partner cafes near you, or the canteens on your campus.

02

Build the order and pay on the spot with UPI or a card.

03

Choose a slot

The kitchen cooks to a 15-minute window, so it is hot when you arrive.

Campus Mode

Built for students, around on-campus canteens and the cafes just outside the gate, where a lunch break is measured in minutes.

QuePoints

Rewards that accumulate on repeat orders, so the regulars who keep a cafe running get something back for it.

Partner dashboard

Cafes see live orders, peak hours, their most popular items, and how customers come back. Demand becomes something you can plan for.

Onboarding in 24–48 hours

Cafe applications are reviewed within two working days. A valid FSSAI licence is required before going live.

How I work

Solve a problem you have had

I lost my own lunch breaks to this queue before I wrote a line of code about it. It makes the first hundred decisions much easier.

Pickup before delivery

Delivery is a logistics company wearing an app. Pickup is a scheduling problem, and scheduling can be solved in software alone.

The partner side is the product

A cafe that cannot see its own peak hours will stop opening the dashboard by the second week. The owner has to win too.

Students first

Campus is where a queue costs the most, and where people will tell you immediately when something does not work.
